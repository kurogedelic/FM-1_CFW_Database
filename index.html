About

Hi, I'm the author of Felucca.

I'd stepped away from FM-1 custom firmware for a while. When I came back, there were so many projects that I got lost — so I made this site to sort them out.

Each project links to its author's own page, along with their support links where available. If you enjoy someone's work, please support them directly.

For your safety, always install firmware from the author's own page. Every project here links to it.

Thank you to Charles, keremimo, genkigenki, juanjiant-bit and isod89 for supporting my work.

I wrote about making Felucca on note: FM-1のCFW「Felucca」を作った話 (Japanese)

— Leo Kuroshita
